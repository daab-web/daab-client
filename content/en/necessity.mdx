# **The Need to Establish the World Association of Azerbaijani Scientists**

---

<Image src={n1} alt="Founding necessity" />

On 22 April 2022, the city of Shusha—the pearl of Karabakh—hosted the
5th Congress of World Azerbaijanis. Considered one of the most
significant milestones in the history of independent Azerbaijan, the
congress brought together almost 400 compatriots from 65 countries. The
comprehensive speech delivered by the President of the Republic, Supreme
Commander-in-Chief Ilham Aliyev, at one of the largest events held in
the liberated territories, placed great responsibilities on our rapidly
developing diaspora. Acting on the President's instructions, purposeful
work is being carried out to ensure better organization of the diaspora
today. The active participation of scientists and academics in the
Azerbaijani diaspora, which stands out for its diversity and potential,
promises major opportunities for its further development. Throughout and
after the congress, we met many new Azerbaijani scholars and academics,
explored possibilities for future collaboration, and shared ideas.

<Image src={n2} alt="Founding necessity 2" />

With the support of the State Committee on Work with the Diaspora of the
Republic of Azerbaijan, a group of Azerbaijani intellectuals focused on
the need to organize the scientists represented in our diaspora. We set
out to create a shared platform that would unite all of us. To realize
this intention, we convened in Türkiye and laid the foundation for the
World Association of Azerbaijani Scientists (WAAS).

The meeting took place on 9 December 2022 in Istanbul with the
participation of representatives of the State Committee and Azerbaijani
academics who had travelled from different countries.
