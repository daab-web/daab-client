**The World Association of Azerbaijani Scientists** aims to become an international organization recognized for excellence in advancing scientific knowledge, promoting innovation, and nurturing scholars, engineers, artists, and healthcare professionals who can make a meaningful impact in Azerbaijan and around the world. We want Azerbaijan to be known and acknowledged globally as a peace-loving nation with a rich and profound history that invests heavily in creating a prosperous and peaceful future for humanity. The world will benefit from Azerbaijan's participation on the global stage, and in turn Azerbaijan will draw on international experience to continue building a stronger state for future generations.
